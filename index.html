About the Platform

Hands-On Before You're On-Site

PhaseShift Education is a professional-grade 3D electrical training platform built for the utility industry. Our interactive simulations put workers inside real-world scenarios — giving them the hands-on experience they need before ever touching live equipment.

Traditional training tells you how it works.
PhaseShift shows you.

Designed for lineworkers, apprentices, and training coordinators, our platform delivers immersive learning that sticks. Every simulation is built to mirror real field conditions.

When workers step on the job site, they're not learning for the first time — they're applying what they already know. That's the PhaseShift difference.
